## Canada Simulator open-data expansion (Aug 2026)
The Canada Simulator now separates high-frequency public feeds from official socioeconomic releases. The province-aware fundamentals layer queries Statistics Canada WDS/CMHC-backed tables for labour-force conditions, provincial GDP, labour productivity, median after-tax income, housing starts, building permits, quarterly population, interprovincial migration, domestic exports and health-sector employment. National-only series are labelled Canada-wide and unsupported geographies are not silently substituted.
